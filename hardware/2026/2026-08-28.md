## Notes

* Approved last week's meeting minutes.

* Discussed QUDT unit changes. Changes were accepted.

* Discussed adding **Model**:

  * [https://github.com/spdx/spdx-3-model/pull/1441/changes](https://github.com/spdx/spdx-3-model/pull/1441/changes)

* Reviewed the following definition from Law Insider:

  * [**Model number**](https://www.lawinsider.com/dictionary/model-number): "a combination of letters, digits, or characters representing the manufacturer, brand, design, or performance of an appliance." The referenced definition also distinguishes the treatment of model numbers for electric motors.

* Product family is a product-family grouping.

* Brands function as categories for marketing. Model number is important, and model and serial numbers are associated.

* Discussed whether both a **family** and **brand** concept are needed and whether they represent different levels of segregation.

* Recall or notice applicability is generally associated with the **model**, rather than the **brand**.

* Questions raised:

  * Should SPDX add a model number?
  * Is a product family also needed?
  * Should there be a model name represented as a string?
  * How should manufacturer, product number, and model hierarchy be represented?
  * A human-readable definition of product number is needed.
  * How should product variants be represented?
