### AI overview: Greg’s chat comments

**How They Relate**

- **Model name** → Human-readable title of the product/process.
- **Model number** → Code for the overall product/version.
- **Part number** → Code for a specific component or variant within that model.

**Analogy:**

- Model name = “Toyota Camry”
- Model number = “Camry 2025 XLE”
- Part number = “Door handle assembly, P/N: DH-25XLE-01” [askai.glarity.app+1](https://www.bing.com/ck/a?!&p=b0688499c2149b8b816a3a158324152a913b917910178ab7ae866fd8bf44f458JmltdHM9MTc5MDI5NDQwMA&ptn=3&ver=2&hsh=4&fclid=299f7cfc-7913-6134-34bf-6bae78b2609e&u=a1aHR0cHM6Ly9hc2thaS5nbGFyaXR5LmFwcC9zZWFyY2gvV2hhdC1pcy1hLXBhcnQtbnVtYmVyLWFuZC1ob3ctZG9lcy1pdC1kaWZmZXItZnJvbS1hLW1vZGVsLW51bWJlcg&ntb=1)

**Model Name**

- **Definition:** The descriptive, often marketing-oriented name of a product, service, or process.
- **Purpose:** Communicates the type or category of the offering in a human-readable way.
- **Example in PMBOK context:** “Project Management Body of Knowledge” is the model name for the PMBOK Guide [Wikipedia](https://www.bing.com/ck/a?!&p=8a656c4dd3ca9f0fda020e07e363314c757f8076aa4eeb4b76949712139d8daeJmltdHM9MTc5MDI5NDQwMA&ptn=3&ver=2&hsh=4&fclid=299f7cfc-7913-6134-34bf-6bae78b2609e&u=a1aHR0cHM6Ly9lbi53aWtpcGVkaWEub3JnL3dpa2kvUHJvamVjdF9NYW5hZ2VtZW50X0JvZHlfb2ZfS25vd2xlZGdl&ntb=1).
- **Key point:** It’s not a code; it’s a label.

**Model Number**

- **Definition:** A unique alphanumeric code assigned to a specific version or configuration of a product or process [askai.glarity.app+1](https://www.bing.com/ck/a?!&p=b0688499c2149b8b816a3a158324152a913b917910178ab7ae866fd8bf44f458JmltdHM9MTc5MDI5NDQwMA&ptn=3&ver=2&hsh=4&fclid=299f7cfc-7913-6134-34bf-6bae78b2609e&u=a1aHR0cHM6Ly9hc2thaS5nbGFyaXR5LmFwcC9zZWFyY2gvV2hhdC1pcy1hLXBhcnQtbnVtYmVyLWFuZC1ob3ctZG9lcy1pdC1kaWZmZXItZnJvbS1hLW1vZGVsLW51bWJlcg&ntb=1).
- **Purpose:** Distinguishes between different versions within the same product line; often encodes technical specifications.
- **Example:** For a printer, “TM-U220” is the model number [POSGuys.com](https://www.bing.com/ck/a?!&p=93a69438c96901401be65edc4bc83acea877c150e78f57ff94cc7788a168b9d6JmltdHM9MTc5MDI5NDQwMA&ptn=3&ver=2&hsh=4&fclid=299f7cfc-7913-6134-34bf-6bae78b2609e&u=a1aHR0cHM6Ly9wb3NndXlzLmNvbS9ibG9nL2thcmx5cy1hZHZpY2UvYXJ0aWNsZS9LYXJseQ&ntb=1).
- **In PMBOK:** The 7th edition (2021) and 8th edition (2025) of the PMBOK Guide are “model numbers” for the body of knowledge, identifying the edition.

**Part Number**

- **Definition:** A unique identifier for a specific component, material, or sub-assembly within a product or process [askai.glarity.app+1](https://www.bing.com/ck/a?!&p=b0688499c2149b8b816a3a158324152a913b917910178ab7ae866fd8bf44f458JmltdHM9MTc5MDI5NDQwMA&ptn=3&ver=2&hsh=4&fclid=299f7cfc-7913-6134-34bf-6bae78b2609e&u=a1aHR0cHM6Ly9hc2thaS5nbGFyaXR5LmFwcC9zZWFyY2gvV2hhdC1pcy1hLXBhcnQtbnVtYmVyLWFuZC1ob3ctZG9lcy1pdC1kaWZmZXItZnJvbS1hLW1vZGVsLW51bWJlcg&ntb=1).
- **Purpose:** Enables precise identification, inventory control, and ordering of individual items.
- **Example:** Within the TM-U220 printer model, “P/N: 12345-ABC” might be the part number for the paper feed assembly [POSGuys.com](https://www.bing.com/ck/a?!&p=93a69438c96901401be65edc4bc83acea877c150e78f57ff94cc7788a168b9d6JmltdHM9MTc5MDI5NDQwMA&ptn=3&ver=2&hsh=4&fclid=299f7cfc-7913-6134-34bf-6bae78b2609e&u=a1aHR0cHM6Ly9wb3NndXlzLmNvbS9ibG9nL2thcmx5cy1hZHZpY2UvYXJ0aWNsZS9LYXJseQ&ntb=1).
- **In PMBOK:** A “part number” could be the code for a specific process artifact, template, or deliverable within the PMBOK framework.
