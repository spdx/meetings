- Functional Safety (FuSa) and Systems Engineering:
  - Discussed the relevance of CRA requirements to Functional Safety.
  - Suggested that the FuSa group expand its focus to systems engineering.
  - Emphasized the need for greater rigor in security, functional safety, and systems engineering.
  - Noted that traditional systems engineering practices have not always incorporated the level of rigor required for functional safety and security.
  - Identified an inflection point where increasing regulatory and technical requirements, including CRA, necessitate greater collaboration among security, functional safety, and systems engineering disciplines.
  - Recognized that these requirements affect the SPDX Core, Software, Hardware, and other profiles.
  - Observed that systems engineering encompasses nearly all functional safety elements.
  - Identified systems engineering as a universal principle applicable across SPDX profiles.
  - Noted that the Operations profile includes aspects of systems engineering, with a greater emphasis on business processes.
  - Recognized supply chain management as a fundamental component of all systems.
  - Discussed the importance of systems engineering for conformance checking, particularly where multiple SPDX profiles are involved.
